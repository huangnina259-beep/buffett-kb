什么是护城河？巴菲特是怎么评估的？ What is an economic moat, and how does Buffett evaluate it?
护城河是企业相对竞争对手的持久竞争优势，使其能保护长期利润。巴菲特在 1995 年股东信中将其归纳为四类来源：品牌溢价、转换成本、网络效应与成本优势… A moat is a company's durable competitive advantage that protects long-term profits. Buffett identifies four sources in his 1995 letter: brand pricing power, switching costs, network effects, and cost advantages…
